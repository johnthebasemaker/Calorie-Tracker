            Features
            
    
            
      
            Both are off to begin with, so the app starts as a plain food and water tracker.
        Turning one off only hides it — nothing you have logged is deleted, and it all comes back when you switch it on again.
            

      

      
    
            

    
            My profile optional
            
    
            
      
            Fill this in and your targets are calculated from your own body, activity and goal
        instead of generic adult values. Skip it and the app works exactly as it does now.
            

      
            
        
        
        
      
            
      
            Update your weight weekly and tap Recalculate — that is what keeps the numbers honest.
            

      

      
      
            

      
      
            
        
        
      
            
      
            

      
      
            
      
      
            

      
      
            
      
            

      
      
      
            
    
            

    
            Daily targets
            
    
            
      
      
      
      
      
      
            
      
            

      
      
            
        
            
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
            Added sugar is the one worth limiting — table sugar, jaggery, honey,
          syrup. Natural sugar is the lactose in milk and the fructose in fruit; there is no
          health guideline restricting it, so its ceiling is for reference only and never turns red.
          Total sugar is the raw figure the other two are worked out from and has no target.
          Clear any box to switch that flag off.
            
      
            

      
      
    
            

    
            Food regions
            
    
            
      
            Switch on the cuisines you actually eat — they stack, so South Indian,
        Gulf and Filipino can all be on at once. A region is downloaded the first time you
        switch it on and then kept on this device, so switching it back on later is instant
        and works with no signal. Switching one off hides its foods from search; nothing you
        have logged is affected.
            
      
            
      
            Anything outside these five still works: search Open Food Facts, estimate
        it with AI, or add it by hand — once saved it is in your library permanently.
            
    
            

    
            
    
            Burn check-in times
            
    
            
      
            When to be reminded to log your burned total. These are nudges, not slots —
        a reading is stamped with the real time you took it, whatever the reminder said. Add as
        many or as few as suit your shift; with none set, burn tracking still works and simply
        never nudges you.
            
      
            
      
      
    
            
    
            

    
            Workout reminders
            
    
            
      
            There is no server, so nothing can be pushed to your phone. These times are
        checked when you open the app: if one has passed and you have not ticked anything off,
        the Workout tab shows a reminder. Add as many as suit your shifts.
            
      
            
      
    
            

    
            
    
            AI estimation
            
    
            
      
            Used only when a food isn’t in Open Food Facts or your library. Your key is stored
        in this browser alone — it is never committed to the repo and never sent anywhere except OpenRouter.
        Get one free at openrouter.ai/keys.
            
      
      
      
            
      
      
      
    
            
    
            

    
            Backup
            
    
            
      
            Everything lives in this browser only. iOS can clear website data, so export a backup now and then.
            
      
      
      
    
            

    
            Danger zone
            
    
            
      
    
            

    
            Built-in nutrition values are averages for home-style cooking — good enough to trend on, not lab-exact. Edit any food to match how yours is actually made.
